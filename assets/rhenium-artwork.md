# Rhenium artwork

Mint Orbit was selected by the user on 16 October 2026. `icon.svg` preserves
that approved mark; `rhenium-monochrome.svg` is the same geometry in one color.
`rhenium-tv-banner.svg` is the editable counterpart of the native TV banner.
The native drawables use identical paths and flat fills. No generated bitmap
is shipped as a logo.

The banner wordmark is outlined from Noto Sans Bold, copyright 2010, 2012–2020
Google Inc./Google LLC, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts . Only the rendered wordmark
outlines are included; no font binary is bundled. The native Home wordmark
continues to use Android's scalable system sans font.

The launcher foreground is inset 18dp within Android's 108dp adaptive layer.
Its visible mark fits inside the 66dp center safe circle. The legacy icon and
160×90dp (320×180 viewport) TV banner retain their existing resource names.
Android TV does not use themed icons; the standalone monochrome export is
available for other brand uses.
