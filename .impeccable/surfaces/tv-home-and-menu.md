Mode: Operate. Scope: native Android TV Home, the browser action drawer, and the first-class Phone entry. The phone companion keeps its existing visual system.

first action and a quiet right-side menu.

**OWN-WORLD:** Forest blue, warm white and pale mint focus; native TV sans
typography and filled controls. One credited photograph supplies the scenery.

**STORY:** Search, open a native favorite, resume a native tab, or connect a phone.

security ownership, D-pad activation and Back restoration remain authoritative.

carry expression while native TV legibility sets the quality bar.
